What the text does not say (doctrinal review notes)

  • Joseph's parents are never named in the book (122:7.4 mentions only that his father "had been recently disabled"). Joseph therefore stands without a parent link — the dashed gold line from Zadoc marks the one stated connection: Zadoc adopted Joseph's paternal orphan ancestor six generations back (122:4.3).
  • Mary's siblings: "two brothers and two sisters" (122:3.3); only Salome is named. The other three are omitted rather than invented.
  • Mary's ancestral women (Annon, Tamar, Ruth, Bathsheba, Ansie, Cloa, Eve, Enta, Ratta — 122:1.2): the text gives no order or parent–child chain, so they are shown unlinked below the tree.
  • Amosad's descent from Seth's line is not stated (78:5.1, 92:5.9); he is shown unlinked under the Sethite tree.
  • Adamson and Ratta's sixty-seven children (77:5.6) are unnamed in the text — including the unique children who originated the secondary midwayers — so no nodes are drawn for them.
  • Joseph's deep ancestry "back to the days of Abraham ... to Andon and Fonta" (122:1.1) refers to racial stocks, not named individuals; it is recorded in his panel, not drawn. The book explicitly corrects the biblical genealogies: David and Solomon are not in Joseph's direct line, and his lineage does not go directly back to Adam (122:1.1).
