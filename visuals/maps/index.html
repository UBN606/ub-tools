Projection. Linear latitude/longitude to SVG: x = (lon − 34) / 2.6 × 800, y = (33.8 − lat) / 3.0 × 1000. Coastline, rivers, and lakes are hand-drawn and schematic — not to scale.

Accuracy. Coordinates position the dots only; they are not claims from the book.
